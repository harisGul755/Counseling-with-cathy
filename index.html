Meet Cathy

I specialize in the treatment of Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD), OCD Spectrum Disorders, and anxiety-related conditions using Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP), the gold-standard, evidence-based approach for OCD.

My intention is to create a space where you can speak openly about your experiences, even the thoughts, fears, or urges that feel most distressing or difficult to discuss. Together, we will work to reduce the power OCD has over your life while helping you develop a different relationship with uncertainty, anxiety, and unwanted thoughts.

My Approach

With over 13 years of clinical experience and advanced training and certification in ERP, I offer structured, collaborative treatment tailored to your specific symptom patterns, values, and goals. Therapy is active and intentional, focused on helping you gradually face feared thoughts or situations while learning to respond differently to anxiety.

My goal is to help you stop organizing your life around OCD and start reconnecting with what matters most to you. I welcome individuals of all identities and backgrounds. If this resonates with you, I’d be honored to walk alongside you in this process.
