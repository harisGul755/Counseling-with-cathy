Frequently Asked Questions

The first session will be focused on getting to know each other and establishing our therapeutic relationship. I will ask you questions about your background and experiences so I can understand you better. We will explore what brings you to therapy and establish goals to work on together. You are always in control of what we do and don’t talk about.

You can schedule an appointment by filling out the form on the contact page, emailing me at connect@counselingwithcathy.com, or using the online scheduling through Headway by clicking here.

The consultation call is a chance for us to talk and see if I would be a good fit for your needs. Research shows one of the most important factors of successful therapy is the therapeutic relationship, so I want to make sure you have a chance to chat with me before making a decision to book a session.

I’m in-network with most major insurance companies including Aetna, Anthem Blue Cross and Blue Shield, Oscar, Oxford, United Healthcare.

If I’m not in network with your insurance, I will provide you with the paperwork you’ll need to file with them for possible reimbursement. Learn more about navigating out-of-network benefits and reimbursement here.
