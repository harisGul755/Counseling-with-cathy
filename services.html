Understanding you

We start by getting a clear picture of your specific symptom patterns, values, and goals.

Exposure-based interventions

Facing fears, step by step

Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP), the gold-standard, evidence-based approach for OCD.

  • Gradually facing feared thoughts or situations, at a pace we agree on.
  • Learning to respond differently to anxiety rather than performing rituals.
  • Building a different relationship with uncertainty, anxiety, and unwanted thoughts.
